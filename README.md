# 📊 Customer Purchase & Retention Analysis

A Python-based customer purchase and sales analysis project designed to understand customer behavior, product performance, repeat purchasing, and monthly sales trends.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze customer purchasing behavior and identify key business insights from order-level sales data.

The analysis focuses on:

* Overall sales performance
* Customer purchasing behavior
* Repeat vs one-time customers
* Product performance
* Monthly revenue and quantity trends
* Business-focused visualizations

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib

### Task 1 — Data Overview

* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Total Products
* Total Quantity Sold
* Total Revenue
* First Order Date
* Last Order Date

### Task 2 — Customer Performance

For each customer:

* Number of Orders
* Total Quantity
* Total Revenue
* Highest Revenue Customer
* Lowest Revenue Customer
* Highest Quantity Customer
* Most Frequent Customer

### Task 3 — Repeat Customer Analysis

* Repeat Customers
* One-time Customers
* Repeat Customer Rate

### Task 4 — Product Performance

* Product-wise Revenue
* Product-wise Quantity
* Best Revenue Product
* Highest-selling Product

### Task 5 — Monthly Sales Analysis

* Monthly Revenue
* Monthly Quantity
* Best-performing Month

### Task 6 — Data Visualization

Created visualizations for:

* Customer vs Revenue

# 🔍 Main Observations

1. **C001 was the strongest customer by revenue**, generating 140,400 in total revenue and also placing the highest number of orders.

2. **C005 purchased the highest quantity of products**, with 21 units sold, despite generating less revenue than the other customers.

3. **Laptop was the best revenue-generating product**, contributing 330,000 in revenue, while **Mouse was the highest-selling product by quantity**, with 41 units sold.

4. **March was the strongest sales month**, generating 216,600 in revenue and 28 units sold, making it the highest-performing month in the analyzed period.

---

# 💡 Business Insight

The analysis shows that revenue performance and purchase volume do not always follow the same pattern. High-value products such as Laptops can generate substantial revenue with relatively low quantities, while lower-priced products such as Mouse can achieve much higher sales volume.

The 100% repeat customer rate also indicates that every customer in this dataset made more than one purchase during the analyzed period.
